Common questions

What multi-site businesses ask us.

Do all our locations need the same hardware?

No. We favor consistent support and security standards, but hardware can differ when site requirements justify it. We document exceptions rather than forcing every location into an unsuitable setup.

Can you work with our existing POS and internet providers?

Yes. We can investigate network, workstation, and connectivity issues and coordinate with the relevant vendor. Proprietary application support and payment processing remain with their providers unless otherwise agreed.

Can you support locations without an on-site IT employee?

Many routine management and troubleshooting tasks can be handled remotely. Some issues require hands-on assistance, vendor visits, or separately arranged on-site service. The agreement establishes coverage and response expectations.

What changes when we add another location?

We review the new site's devices, network, vendors, support needs, and security requirements. Additional recurring coverage and any setup project are scoped and priced according to the agreement.

Can we start with one location?

Yes. An initial site can help establish a practical support and security baseline before discussing broader rollout, if that fits your business.

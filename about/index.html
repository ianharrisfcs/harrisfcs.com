About HarrisFCS

Built for businesses worth preserving.

HarrisFCS is a family-owned technology company founded in Oregon in 2019. We help small businesses manage their technology, strengthen their security, and meet compliance obligations without surrendering control of their operations to an unnecessarily complicated technology stack.

Why the company exists

Technology should strengthen and enable businesses to reach their full potential.

We believe healthy businesses are built by people with real ownership, responsibility, and knowledge of their work. Technology should strengthen their operability; not make an organization more dependent, more centralized, or less capable of understanding the systems it relies on.

That principle shapes how we work. We favor durable solutions over unnecessary complexity, long-term relationships over transactional sales, and tools appropriate to the actual size and needs of the organization. We would rather help a client build something resilient than sell them more "solutions".

HarrisFCS itself is built, and growing, with the same philosophy. Our goal is not growth for its own sake. We want to build a durable family business that provides meaningful work, supports families, develops capable people, serves other independent businesses, and remains accountable to the communities and clients from which it grows.

How we work

Practical, direct, accountable.

  • Security is considered from the beginning, not bolted on later.
  • Compliance work includes implementation, not just documentation.
  • We recommend what the environment needs, not what creates the largest software stack.
  • Clients have direct access to the people responsible for the work.
The operating philosophy

Technology should become boring in the right ways.

The goal is not to make IT impressive. The goal is to make the business less fragile.

Manage what exists

Know the devices, users, systems, patches, backups, and support needs already inside the business.

Secure what matters

Apply stronger controls where data sensitivity, business impact, or regulatory obligations justify them.

Govern the environment

Document responsibilities, review changes, and make sure the written rules continue to match reality.

A good fit

We work best with organizations that want ownership, not excuses.

HarrisFCS is a strong fit when the business wants one accountable partner to help manage the environment and is willing to make reasonable changes when risk or reliability requires them.

Careers & Collaboration

Do good work. Take ownership. Build something that lasts.

HarrisFCS is an independent, family-owned IT company. We're interested in people who care about their craft, treat clients with respect, and want to develop real capability—not just collect job titles.

What matters here

Competence, responsibility, and relationships.

Own the outcome

Take responsibility for understanding the problem, communicating clearly, and seeing work through. Ask for help when you need it; don't hide uncertainty.

Learn the underlying systems

We value people who want to understand why technology works, not just memorize the next button to click. Curiosity and sound judgment matter.

Respect the client

Our clients are running real businesses. Their time, budgets, staff, and trust deserve practical recommendations and honest communication.

The company we're building

A durable business, not an exit strategy.

We want HarrisFCS to remain an independent business that provides meaningful work, develops capable people, supports families, and serves its community and clients over the long term.

That means favoring thoughtful solutions over unnecessary complexity, clear accountability over handoffs, and relationships over transactional sales.

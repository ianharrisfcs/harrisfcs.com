Privacy

Website privacy.

HarrisFCS collects only the information reasonably needed to respond to inquiries and operate this website.

Information you provide

If you submit a contact form, email us, or call us, we may receive information such as your name, company, email address, phone number, and the information you choose to provide about your technology needs.

How we use it

We use inquiry information to respond to you, evaluate whether HarrisFCS can help, provide requested services, and maintain business records related to that relationship. We do not sell contact-form information.

Website hosting and forms

This website is hosted using third-party infrastructure. Form submissions may be processed by our hosting provider and then made available to HarrisFCS for follow-up.

Security

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Do not submit passwords, Social Security numbers, protected health information, taxpayer documents, or other highly sensitive data through the general website contact form.

Third-party links

The site may link to external services such as WISPCheck or vendor resources. Those services may have their own privacy practices.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact office@harrisfcs.com.

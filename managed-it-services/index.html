Four service levels

Land. Manage. Secure. Govern.

The tiers are not arbitrary good/better/best bundles. Essential is a low-friction managed baseline. Preferred is the normal fit for general business IT. Premier is for stronger voluntary security maturity. Strategic adds the governance and compliance work regulated environments require.

Essential

Land
Foundational managed IT

24/7 remote monitoring, LAN monitoring, ticketing, automated patching, asset tracking, business-hours remote support, core endpoint protection, backup monitoring when backup is purchased, and monthly patch reporting.

Preferred

Manage
The standard general-business fit

Adds secure remote access, stronger endpoint protection, human-risk intelligence, phishing simulations, security awareness training, dark-web/breach monitoring, quarterly device-health summaries, and security-posture reporting.

Premier

Secure
Security-first managed IT

Adds 24/7 MDR, DNS filtering, mobile-device management, email security, identity and MFA work, password management, cloud productivity suite administration, policy management, extended-hours support, and executive risk reporting.

Strategic

Govern
Security plus compliance governance

Adds WISP and IRS Publication 4557 work, FTC Safeguards advisory, HIPAA Security Risk Assessment support, annual compliance review, a data-security platform, custom reporting, and dedicated vCISO advisory.

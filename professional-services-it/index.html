Why businesses call us

You shouldn't have to become an IT expert to run your business.

“We're too small for managed IT.”

Even a handful of computers can carry essential customer information and daily operations. The right level of support depends on your actual needs, not an arbitrary company size.

“We already have someone who fixes computers.”

Reactive repairs are useful. Ongoing management adds maintenance, visibility, security practices, and planning before issues become emergencies.

“We don't want a complicated contract.”

We explain recurring service scope, available options, and what would require a separate project before the work begins.

“We can't afford to be down.”

Monitoring, updates, recovery planning, and an established support relationship can reduce avoidable disruptions. No system is immune to failure, but preparation matters.

Recurring IT services maintain and support an agreed existing environment. Projects deliver a defined change, such as a migration, network redesign, or major equipment rollout.

We'll explain which applies and discuss separately billable work in advance. Your service agreement determines what's included.

FAQ

Common questions about outside IT support.

Do we need an internal IT employee?

Not necessarily. HarrisFCS can provide ongoing external IT management and support, with scope tailored to the business. Some organizations also use us alongside internal staff.

Can you work with our existing software vendors?

Yes. We can investigate the underlying IT issues and coordinate with application vendors, while respecting their responsibility for specialized software.

What if we only need a one-time fix or upgrade?

We can discuss a defined IT project instead of assuming recurring managed services are the right fit.

How do you decide what to recommend?

We start with the systems your business depends on, the risks and recurring problems, and your budget. Recommendations should have a clear operational purpose.

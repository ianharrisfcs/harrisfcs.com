Managed service levels

Four levels built for four different business realities.

We do not publish a one-size-fits-all number before discovery. The service level is selected around the environment, risk, support needs, and regulatory obligations.

Essential

Land
Foundational managed IT.

24/7 monitoring, patching, asset tracking, business-hours remote support, endpoint protection, and core reporting.

Preferred

Manage
The normal fit for general business IT.

Adds secure remote access, stronger endpoint security, human-risk intelligence, phishing simulations, awareness training, and breach monitoring.

Premier

Secure
For organizations that take security seriously.

Adds 24/7 MDR, DNS filtering, mobile device management, email security, MFA and identity work, password management, policy controls, and executive risk reporting.

Strategic

Govern
For regulated and governance-heavy environments.

Adds WISP and IRS Publication 4557 work, FTC Safeguards advisory, HIPAA assessment support, annual compliance review, data-security platform, and dedicated vCISO advisory.

A plan is only useful when the business actually follows it.

The IRS continues to remind tax professionals that federal law requires them to create and maintain a WISP. The plan should address employee management and training, information systems, and detecting and managing system failures.

HarrisFCS goes beyond drafting. We help align the environment with the plan and maintain the controls that support it.

  • Endpoint protection / MDR
